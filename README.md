# Interoceptomics

How does the body sense a heartbeat or a breath, and how do the neural connections involved carry that information? Interoceptomics is a proposed field bringing those questions together: the study of internal-body sensing and its relationships with neural connectivity.

[Download the presentation and scripts](https://github.com/eliminatenoise/Interoceptomics/releases/tag/interoceptomics-r42-draft). This is a working draft: the illustrations describe proposed concepts, and scientific and native playback reviews remain incomplete.
