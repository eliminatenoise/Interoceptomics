Interoceptomics · experimental preview · scripts only

Start with a signal you can measure.

What lets you feel a heartbeat or a breath, and how are the neural connections involved arranged? Interoceptomics brings internal-body sensing and neural connectivity into one proposed research programme. These scripts develop the explanations, measurements and experiments behind its presentation.

This partial preview contains the existing provisional scripts, unchanged. Their newer slide deck is excluded because exact image permissions remain unresolved. The scripts retain references to those absent illustrations. Older presentation and model downloads are historical editions and do not form a matching package with these scripts.

The programme and its illustrations do not establish a completed map of experience, validated clinical interfaces or demonstrated skill transfer. Scientific, anatomical, model and full visual checks remain incomplete. Development remains paused while NeuroSurge is the active project; publication makes the existing work available for inspection.
