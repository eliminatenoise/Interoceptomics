Interoceptomics

A map of the wiring is not a map of what the body senses.

Interoceptomics is a proposed field of science: interoception, the body sensing its own interior (a heartbeat, a breath, a muscle at work), joined to connectomics, the mapping of nerve connections. Function first, and from function, structure: measure what the body senses and does, then ask which connections carry it.

A 3D atlas of the human brain that you turn, cut and measure

This is the piece you can use today. It shows the left half of the Allen Institute's human reference atlas, 141 named regions on a half-millimetre grid, and colours the regions linked to moving and feeling the body, to running its organs, and to both at once. Lift a region out, cut through any point, measure between two points.

Download the atlas viewer ZIP, 40.6 MB

  1. Extract the whole ZIP into a folder.
  2. Start the viewer with the file for your system: open Windows.cmd on Windows or macOS.command on macOS, or run sh Linux.sh in a terminal on Linux.
  3. Your browser opens the atlas at an address on your own computer. If it stays closed, copy the address the start file prints into the browser. Press Ctrl+C in that window when you are done.

You need Python 3.10 or newer and a current browser. Nothing is installed. The viewer has been run on Linux, in Chromium; the Windows and macOS start files have not yet been tried on those systems.

The viewer is an illustration built on a published atlas. It shows one averaged reference brain, left half only. Its colours are teaching associations chosen from cited sources, not recorded activity. Nothing in it has been validated, scientifically or clinically.

The talk is paused; its scripts are published

The proposal is argued in a talk, I Know Kung Fu: 111 slides on what it would take to read a movement out of one nervous system and teach it to another. Slides 87 and 88 propose Interoceptomics as a field. The talk is paused. Its two scripts follow the same slides.

  • Technical script, 114 pages, every step argued in full with references: PDF · DOCX
  • Simple script, 36 pages, the essential spoken lines: PDF · DOCX

The slides are not published, because 22 of their 88 pictures have not been cleared for redistribution; the scripts still describe those pictures. The experiments in the talk are proposals, and none has been run. About the two scripts

One programme, three projects

Interoceptomics belongs to Brain2Body, a programme about connecting brain and body function. NeuroTranslation asks how well one recording of the body predicts another, on open human recordings. NeuroSurge is a browser game for learning which motor areas of the brain move which parts of the body; its activity is simulated.
