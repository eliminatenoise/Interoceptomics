# One week later

Raeed H. Chowdhury, Joshua I. Glaser and Lee E. Miller recorded the same rhesus macaque, animal C, on 7 and 14 September 2017. These excerpts come from the two-workspace reaching task. The processed files identify the animal; they give no age for C. The original authors released the data under CC0 1.0. The forecasts and viewer are a secondary analysis by the Interoceptomics and NeuroTranslation projects. [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.nk98sf7q7) · [Original research](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.48198) · [CC0](../CC0-1.0.txt)

## Read one reach

A camera tracked Marker_1 on the hand and Pronation_Pt1 on the proximal ulna near the elbow. They share a shoulder-relative Cartesian frame in metres. The robot handle has an independent planar encoder frame in centimetres. A sphere marks each measured site. Its radius, the straight connector and the grid are illustrations. The glTF files use metres throughout, mapping optical (X, Y, Z) to (X, Z, −Y) and planar (X, Y) to (X/100, 0, −Y/100). The two coordinate systems keep separate origins. [Source marker processing](https://github.com/raeedcho/s1-kinematics/blob/e6363cf724d88467d06d472563221a9c85d4dd1b/lib/lib-two-workspace/mwEncoders.m) · [Source coordinate registration](https://github.com/limblab/KinectTracking/blob/f656ab81dd0ea2072c642f02afbe8f8fc472c3db/functions/transformForOpenSim.m)

The original controller recorded a go word 50 ms before the forecast cutoff, row k. The twenty 10 ms bins k−20 through k−1 provide earlier positions, go history and cortical activity. Ten measured positions then occupy k through k+9. At the second clock boundary, k+10, the last movement sample is 10 ms old and the last spike bin used by the primary forecast began 110 ms earlier. Both count forecasts target the still-future ten bins k+10 through k+19. After the closing edge, k+20, all ten bins have elapsed. The viewer displays a selected bin with its start and end, and reveals the observed total when the final bin is selected.

The primary Poisson model predicts a 100 ms count for each acquisition contact. The early model uses the most recent position, coordinate velocity over four intervals, earlier contact totals over 200 ms and earlier go history. An otherwise matched model also receives the difference between the observed endpoint and its constant-velocity forecast, across the six optical and two encoder coordinates. The new position at +90 ms enters the second model. Both exclude spike counts from row k onward. Neither supplies a separate fitted count for individual 10 ms bins. The primary contact comparison pools all exported sorts on the 37 contacts with unsorted coverage in every block. Two sensitivity comparisons use only sort 0 on those contacts or only positive sorts on the 17 contacts with positive coverage throughout.

The motor forecast runs a separate experiment. Three frozen Transformers predict movement from earlier body position and go cues. The day-one residual model corrects that forecast from either earlier pose and cue features alone or from those features and earlier counts pooled by contact. The displayed trajectories average the three fitted seeds, 17, 41 and 73. Each uses only the first 200 ms of the clip. The count update uses a constant-velocity extrapolation and the measured endpoint, independently of the selected motor overlay.

A separate comparison uses the 17 shared positive-sort contacts. It supplies recent spike counts through k+9 to both models. One also receives measured displacements at k+4 and k+9. The two values in the expandable comparison are expected positive-sort totals over k+10 through k+19. The primary experiment and its positive-sort sensitivity exclude these recent spikes.

An electrode number identifies an acquisition channel. Sorting labels identify local clusters. The guide exported 74 columns on 52 contacts on 7 September and 98 columns on 60 contacts in each later block. Both days represented 38 contacts. Stable neuron identities, detector gains and an independent electrode remapping log are unavailable. A missing sort remains absent. A zero is a count of zero on a represented column. These counts measure cortex and give no direct recording of a peripheral afferent. Shared offline timing, marker interpolation, filtering and offline sorting limit any inference about causal or real-time feedback. ## Reproduce the five excerpts

The training date uses its near-workspace training originals. The later date contributes two physical acquisition blocks, each containing near and far trials. Original trial identities restart in the second block. For each represented block × workspace take the first candidate by original chronology, then its first eligible later go. Fix those identities from controller timing before inspecting positions or counts. The five examples have no earlier different eligible original in their own block, workspace and go ordinal. The mismatched-movement control belongs to the matched cohort and has no example value here.
