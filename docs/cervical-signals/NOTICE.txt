Spinal cord and heart surfaces
Kristen Browne and Heidi Schlehlein, HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas, Female united organ set v1.10, based on the National Library of Medicine’s Visible Human Dataset. CC BY 4.0. From the existing Interoceptomics four-organ atlas subset (SHA256 dadce56c2c48bbabda915aae8d2a47bbcb0c2ae81dae6675528279590de7705e), this reader retains the eight cervical segments and heart surfaces. It centres each group, converts the display axes to right/anterior/superior, merges nearby vertices with a 0.9 mm cord grid or a 2.6 mm heart grid, and assigns display colours. The models supply regional landmarks. They have no spatial registration to the study participants, to the head template or to the arm illustration.
