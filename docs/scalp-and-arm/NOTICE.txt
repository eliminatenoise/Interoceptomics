The channel map combines numbers and names from Table 2, with unchanged native
channel labels and per-channel sample rates from the headers of motor execution
subject 1 runs 1–3. The dataset gives the cue codes in Table 1. Codebook and
header differ for lateral eye channels 62 and 64. A dash at 80–83 or 96 is the
published table entry. The header gives names at those indices. The spelling
“litte” in two header labels is preserved. Hz is samples per second. The GDF
unit strings are blank, dimension codes are zero and digital/physical extrema
match. Hardware timing offsets and physical calibration have not been established.
Clock ticks and poses in this reader are computed from the nominal trial schedule.
The optional measured layer summarizes real EEG, EOG and mechanical recordings
from one person, motor execution runs 1–3. Each cue has 18 trials. Values are
summarized within each run before averaging the three results. The aggregates
and their cue-fields.js browser serialization retain CC BY 4.0; the licence
applies to both. See measurements/README.md for methods and reproducibility.
