What happened with real recordings?

The models learned from five people and were tested on five others. Adding ECG improved the forecast for two of them and made it worse for three. The average increase in error was 8.76% relative to each person’s chest-and-breathing forecast. Both fitted models beat simply repeating the last vibration reading in all five people.
