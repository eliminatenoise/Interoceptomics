How the shoulders set the axes

Take 15 frames, ending at frame 0. In each, draw a line from the shoulder on the implant side to the opposite shoulder. Its average direction points along the first axis. Choose the perpendicular that points toward decreasing image y. The final earlier shoulder midpoint marks the origin. One unit is the median shoulder separation across those 15 frames. Freeze all three before looking at a later wrist. Scrubbing the timeline moves the visible arms while these axes stay fixed at the cutoff.

Camera x increases right and y increases down. The shoulder plot puts the opposite-shoulder axis to the right and the image-up perpendicular above the origin. The same projection and scale apply to both arms. The middle of two plotted shoulders on a later frame can move away from this fixed origin.

Missing earlier shoulder coordinates, median width under one pixel, mean shoulder direction shorter than half the median width, or an image-up choice with a vertical component below 0.10 in magnitude withhold the shared frame. Each arm also needs its own 15 earlier wrist, elbow and shoulder pairs. A missing future wrist leaves its forecast eligible and its error unknown. At 30 frames per second, +8 means 0.267 seconds after frame 0 and +15 means 0.500 seconds. Frame 0 is the last pre-onset image in the experiment.

Anatomy, camera and implant references

Peterson and colleagues recorded ordinary movements during epilepsy monitoring. Their camera methods and figures show the tracked shoulders, elbows and wrists. The authors identified reaches on the side opposite the implant. Table 2 places the first person’s implant on the left and the second person’s on the right. Here the side switch illustrates those assignments with two constructed reaches. It gives no information about hand preference. AJILE12 methods, Table 2 and figures 1–3 · Original data release

The shoulder, elbow and wrist follow joint centres in MakeHuman’s artist-built rig. In the upper-extremity model of Holzbaur, Murray and Delp the shoulder, forearm and hand have several rotational degrees of freedom. Our two linked segments preserve their lengths through the reach but omit joint-specific muscle paths, loading and the motion of the shoulder blade. Upper-extremity model and anatomical comparisons · MakeHuman surface and skeleton

Wu and colleagues define three-dimensional joint axes from anatomical landmarks on individual bones. Two shoulder estimates in an image cannot supply those landmarks, camera depth, metres or a joint angle. A dot on the head names an implant hemisphere only. Electrical recordings have their own contact coordinates and clock. Neither contact locations nor voltages enter this example. International Society of Biomechanics joint-coordinate recommendations
