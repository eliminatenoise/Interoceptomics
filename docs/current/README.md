# Interoceptomics experimental script preview

Start with a movement we can measure. Record the relevant signals, capture what the body does, and compare the prediction with that independent reference. These scripts develop that instrument-building problem and the research programme around it.

**This is a partial preview containing four updated script files.** The accompanying presentation is excluded while its exact image permissions are resolved. The scripts retain their existing 111-slide sequence and 135 reveal cues, including descriptions of illustrations that are absent from this download.

Interoceptomics is the project's proposed study connecting internal-body sensing with neural connectivity. The name identifies a research direction; it does not establish a comprehensive or clinically validated map.

## What the preview establishes

These are existing provisional outputs created on 4 November 2026 UTC. All four files are preserved byte for byte, with their original metadata dates. SHA256SUMS.txt and PUBLIC-MANIFEST.json identify this exact selection.

An earlier independent check found ordered body-text agreement between each DOCX and PDF: 1,114 technical paragraphs and 896 simple paragraphs. It accounted explicitly for PDF line wrapping and page footers. That check establishes extracted text agreement. Complete page-by-page visual review, native Office playback and other-machine layout remain incomplete.

The scripts explain proposed experiments and distinguish them from cited findings. The ape illustration they describe is AI-generated, rather than a measured simulation result. The proposed brain-contact views use simplified, anatomically unregistered models. No transfer of a learned skill was demonstrated by those illustrations.

EEG measures electrical potentials at the scalp. Surface EMG measures muscle electrical activity. Those recordings need their task, timing, recording conditions and an independent movement reference to support a specified prediction. A structural connection does not establish its function, and a movement prediction does not establish a universal neural language or unique private experience.

Clinical safety, anatomical and functional accuracy, held-out performance, uncertainty, reference-series comparisons and complete model validation remain open. The scripts provide research explanations, not a clinical protocol or a validated treatment.
