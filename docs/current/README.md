# The scripts of *I Know Kung Fu*

In *The Matrix*, Neo has kung fu loaded into his brain through a cable. Four words, and years of practice disappear. *I Know Kung Fu* is a talk of 111 slides that asks what a real version would have to measure, and it begins small: can recorded signals predict a movement that was measured separately? Interoceptomics is proposed near its end, on slides 87 and 88.

The talk is paused. These are its two scripts, as they stood on 4 November 2026 (UTC).

## From a film to a field in 111 slides

The names in bold are the talk's own slide titles.

| Slides | Part |
| --- | --- |
| 1–6 | **I Know Kung Fu.** *The Matrix*, a comic-book telepath, and twin sisters whose two brains are connected. What would a working link between nervous systems have to show? |
| 7–13 | **Human Genome Project Failed.** The genome was sequenced, and most rare diseases still have no approved treatment. A Croc is not a crocodile: what a thing looks like does not settle what it does. |
| 14–24 | **Representation, Context, Grounding.** Three questions taken from machine learning. What information did we keep? Which body, task and moment does it belong to? What independent observation could show it is wrong? |
| 25–35 | **Start With Movement.** A movement can be measured from outside, so a prediction about it can fail. |
| 36–74 | **Kung Fu THE HARD WAY.** Six proposed stages in a small model animal (the scripts picture an antechinus, a marsupial mouse): from relating brain recordings to body movement in one task, to passing help from one nervous system to another, to help from a software agent. |
| 75–82 | **Muscle Function First. Then We Do It In Humans.** Sensing tied to muscle (the talk's word is *myoception*), then sensing tied to the organs (*visceroception*). |
| 83–89 | **Starting A New Field.** Two proposed papers, and on slides 87 and 88 the field itself: Interoceptomics, interoception plus connectomics. |
| 90–102 | **Kung Fu THE “EASY” WAY.** Four proposed tasks for people, using recordings from the scalp (EEG), recordings of muscle activity (EMG) and cameras. |
| 103–111 | **Coming Eventually.** Slide 108 is the still picture of what the [atlas viewer](../../README.md) now lets you turn: the brain regions linked to moving the body, to running its organs, and to both. |

## Everything in the scripts is a proposal

The scripts cite published studies. Everything beyond those is proposed: none of the experiments has been run, and no skill has been transferred to anyone.

The scripts describe pictures you cannot see here. The slides are not published. 22 of their 88 pictures are known not to be cleared for redistribution, among them film and character images, a photograph of the twins and a broadcaster's illustration, and the rights to the other 66 have not been confirmed.

EMG is a recording of muscle's electrical activity. It is not a recording of nerves. Where the scripts pair it with a brain recording, they still need a separately measured movement to check any prediction against.
