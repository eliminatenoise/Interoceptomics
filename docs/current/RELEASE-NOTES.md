# Script editions

The technical and simple scripts follow the same 111 slides of *I Know Kung Fu*. Each is available as a PDF and an editable Word document.

| Date | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| 20 November 2026 | The 4 November prerelease was withdrawn. Its deck, scripts and 3D companion are no longer public downloads. |
| 20 November 2026 | In the technical and simple scripts, steps 0.3 and 0.5 now read **NeuroReplay**. The name was previously NeuroRelay. |
| 20 November 2026 | The pages that describe the scripts, the [README](README.md) and this one, were rewritten. That edit left the four script files unchanged. |
| 10 November 2026 | The four script files were published here, without their slides. |

What is known to be missing or unchecked:

- The slides are not published: 22 of their 88 pictures are known not to be cleared for redistribution, and the rights to the other 66 have not been confirmed. The scripts still describe the pictures.
- The PDFs were made with LibreOffice on one computer. The DOCX files have not been opened in Microsoft Word, and their page breaks depend on the fonts installed.
- The scripts argue for experiments. None has been run, and nothing in them is a clinical instruction.

Compared with the 4 November edition, these scripts use the new name on slides 45 and 47 and revised picture descriptions on slides 53, 60 and 62.
