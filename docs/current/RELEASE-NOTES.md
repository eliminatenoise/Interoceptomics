# The scripts have not changed since 10 November 2026

They are the technical and the simple script of *I Know Kung Fu*, each as PDF and DOCX, as produced on 4 November 2026 (UTC). The talk they belong to is paused.

| Date | What changed |
| --- | --- |
| 20 November 2026 | The pages that describe the scripts, the [README](README.md) and this one, were rewritten. The four script files are unchanged. |
| 10 November 2026 | The four script files were published here, without their slides. |

What is known to be missing or unchecked:

- The slides are not published: 22 of their 88 pictures are known not to be cleared for redistribution, and the rights to the other 66 have not been confirmed. The scripts still describe the pictures.
- The PDFs were made with LibreOffice on one computer. The DOCX files have not been opened in Microsoft Word, and their page breaks depend on the fonts installed.
- The scripts argue for experiments. None has been run, and nothing in them is a clinical instruction.

The [pre-release of 4 November 2026](https://github.com/eliminatenoise/Interoceptomics/releases/tag/interoceptomics-r42-draft) holds an older slide deck and the same two scripts one revision earlier. The files here differ from those on slides 53, 60 and 62, where the description of the picture changed.
