Adapted by the Interoceptomics project. Each PNG shows one complete coronal
plane through the adult human annotation on the ICBM 2009b nonlinear symmetric
reference. One pixel represents one 0.5 × 0.5 mm voxel face. Source left is on
the left and superior is up. Orange picks out the exact source-left label.
Grey tones come from the colours of neighbouring atlas labels. A dark
background pixel has source label zero. Source gaps remain visible. The
sections show anatomy. Activity, microscopic nuclei and individual fibres
require separate observations.

The index gives the original voxel coordinates, plane positions, annotation
checksum and image checksums. In each indexed PNG, the entries for context
colours match mapping.paletteIds. Entry 255 stands for the selected source ID.
Rows from the top correspond to decreasing native k, starting at 377. Columns
from the left correspond to increasing native i, starting at zero. Each series
uses three source planes through or near an occupied source-left voxel. The
source-left criterion is a voxel centre at R < 0 mm. For the hypothalamus the
selected ID is 10467. Its separately numbered subdivisions have their own
labels in the surrounding image.
