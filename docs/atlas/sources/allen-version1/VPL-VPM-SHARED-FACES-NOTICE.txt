For every shared face record its perpendicular axis (0, 1 or 2 for R, A or S) and the integer voxel indices i, j, k of the lower-index centre, irrespective of label order. The other centre lies one step higher along that axis. All centres and face corners are left of native R=0. Indices are zero-based. The qform applies 0.5 mm per step and has its first centre at RAS [−98, −134, −72] mm. Each face is halfway between the neighbours, spans 0.5 mm along both transverse axes, and is drawn as two triangles in world metres (−R, S, A)/1000.
