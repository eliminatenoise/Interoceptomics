Direct source label 13230 (PaO), left hemisphere. All 43,319 labelled centres have R < 0. The 0.5 mm isotropic source grid gives 5,414.875 mm³. The unsmoothed Lorensen 0.5 isosurface uses display metres (−R, S, A)/1000 and has added normals and material. It encloses about 5,404.76 mm³ and its area is about 3,102.80 mm². These mesh measures describe the chosen isosurface. The mesh retains a small enclosed hole at one unlabelled voxel.
