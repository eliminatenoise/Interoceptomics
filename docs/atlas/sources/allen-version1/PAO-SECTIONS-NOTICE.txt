Changes
Every full sagittal, coronal and axial plane containing label 13230 is encoded
in assets/parietal-operculum-*.bin within the viewer folder. The annotation IDs
keep their original values, including zero for an unlabelled voxel.
The section index describes the packing, native affine and hashes.
The browser adds an optional 6 mm-padded neighbourhood view, a label palette,
point readout and a registered 3D frame. Source orientation and 0.5 mm voxel
spacing determine the frame. The accompanying population-atlas mesh is the
surface described in PARIETAL-OPERCULUM-NOTICE.txt. Photographer images and MRI are excluded.
