Interoceptomics Atlas Viewer
Version of 21 November 2026


WHAT THIS VERSION IS

The twenty regions that had no surface now have one: the white matter of
the forebrain and five of its named bundles of nerve fibres, nine fluid
spaces (the ventricles and canals), the four regions of the smell
pathway, and the claustrum. Together they hold 2,255,724 of the atlas's
6,999,201 labelled voxels: a third of the atlas.

  Show white matter and fluid spaces. A new button under "Reset view"
  draws fifteen of them in place, in every view: the white matter,
  its fibre bundles and the fluid spaces. The white matter of the forebrain
  and the fluid spaces are drawn see-through, so that the regions inside
  and behind them stay in sight. They are a 36 MB download, so they
  stay off until you ask.

  Show it alone. Each of the twenty can be found in the list under "Find a
  region" and lifted out on its own, like the other regions with a surface
  of their own. Its file loads when you pick it.

  Until you ask, the 3D model looks exactly as before, pixel for pixel;
  the panel has one new button.

The new surfaces were made from the same atlas grid, the same way as the
thirty single-region surfaces of the main model file: a surface around the
region's voxels on the half-millimetre grid, smoothed so that no point
moved more than 0.125 mm. Where a new surface meets a region that already
had one, the two stay within a quarter of a millimetre of each other.
viewer/omissions.html lists the twenty; THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.html says who
made them and what you must keep.

The notes under "Evidence and limits", shown when you select a region,
now say in plain words what the earlier ones said in working shorthand.
A cited source is named by its authors and year instead of a code, each
structure is explained before its abbreviation is given, and the sentence
about how the sources were chosen comes once per region instead of in
front of every note. Each note says what it said before and links to the
same sources.


WHAT IT HAS BEEN RUN ON

Linux, in Chromium 155 with software rendering, both from the start file
and from a copy of the website served on this computer: the first view,
the white matter and fluid spaces in each view, regions shown alone, a
colour overlay, the three flat cuts, and the page opened straight from the
folder. The Windows and macOS start files have not yet been tried on those
systems, and the viewer has not been run on a phone.
