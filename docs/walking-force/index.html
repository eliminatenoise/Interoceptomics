The floor records each step

A force plate is a sensor set into the floor. It records how hard a foot presses down. Cameras record the position of markers on the shoes. Move through one recorded walk and see how the floor load changes as the front of the right foot rises and falls.

Total vertical floor force

Force is shown in newtons (N). Loads below 20 N are treated as unloaded.

Height of the front of the right foot
