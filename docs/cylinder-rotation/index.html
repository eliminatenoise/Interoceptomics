What was in the hand?

A printed cylinder, 50 mm across and 25 mm tall. Two reflective markers sit above the orange sensor. The whole assembly weighed 41 g.

The instrument estimates its orientation on board. Here the first pose sets the reference. The cylinder turns around a fixed centre. The recording supplies no table heading, wrist angle, muscle activity or nerve signals.
