Methods and measurements

Ten 20 ms bins represent preceding force and six measured muscle channels. The native-recurrence checkpoint was fitted at three passes through the middle 12 of its 24 layers. Identical weights generated all five depths. The physical reference extends the latest force and uses a fitted event prior.

The comparison reuses 216 same-tick plate pairs from nine previously scored validation walks by three qualifying participants out of six. People are weighted equally. Force uses 432 rows. The loading error and the joint support use 427 rows. The 18 positive loading rows represent eight confirmed onsets. The final test remains unopened.

On endpoint-specific support, three passes had the lowest impulse MAE at 2.895 N·s versus 3.237 N·s for the physical reference. Five passes reached Brier error 0.038593 versus 0.038625 at three. The page presents precomputed scores. Use the source checkpoint instructions to run the forecaster.
