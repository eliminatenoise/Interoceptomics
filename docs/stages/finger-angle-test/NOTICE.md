# Recordings and analysis

Keshav Bimbraw released the *Forearm Ultrasound Dataset for Hand Gesture Classification and Finger Joint Angle Estimation* under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The original recordings contain synchronized forearm ultrasound and finger tracking. [Dataset record](https://zenodo.org/records/21825018).

Keshav Bimbraw, Christopher J. Nycz, Matthew Schueler, Ziming Zhang and Haichong K. Zhang describe acquisition in *Simultaneous Estimation of Hand Configurations and Finger Joint Angles Using Forearm Ultrasound*, IEEE Transactions on Medical Robotics and Bionics, 2023. [Original publication](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMRB.2023.3237774).

This reader distributes newly computed aggregate prediction errors by anonymous participant, gesture, speed and finger. The study fitted its models on four participants and tested the saved forecasts against measurements from two others. The local JavaScript recomputes the displayed averages from the grouped errors.
