Measured movement and logged command

Ryosuke Tanaka and Ruben Portugues recorded larval zebrafish during externally programmed visual motion. A camera provided a signed tail-bend angle. On a 320 ms grid, the forecast target was the population standard deviation of the angles from the issue time up to, but excluding, 100 ms later. A sustained bend can have little spread. The open-loop program set the pattern direction independently of swimming. Its log records the requested direction on a software clock. Physical screen onset, camera exposure timing and live arrival were unverified. The assay used no neural measurements.

Seven forecasting rules

Carry-forward repeats the pooled spread from the preceding 100 ms. The three linear ridge models correct that starting value using the means and standard deviations from four earlier 50 ms camera bins. Three matched Transformers use attention, which lets the weight given to one reading depend on the other inputs. In each family one model uses camera only, one adds ten direction readings from the preceding five seconds including the issue time, and one shifts those command cutoffs back six seconds. The older control retains real history. Model fitting gave each recording equal total weight. Forecasts of spread were bounded below by zero.

Development and later test

All 29 eligible Figure 4 recordings were assigned by date. Of 17 recordings from 13 April 2023, 13 supplied the initial fit and four checked model selection. The chosen Transformer width was 24. Carry-forward gave the lowest equally weighted error on the four development recordings, 0.023542 radians. After fitting on all 17 April recordings, each model issued 90,227 forecasts on the 12 recordings from 16 May. The later camera target was unusable in 78 windows, leaving 90,149 common cases for scoring.

Command effects depend on the comparator

On the later equal-recording mean, the Transformer with current commands had the lowest error, 0.020223 radians. The camera-only Transformer reached 0.020686 and carry-forward 0.023516. Adding the current command history reduced the camera-only Transformer’s mean error by 0.000463 radians. Compared with carry-forward, the camera-only Transformer reduced mean error by 0.002830 radians. The full camera-plus-current-command Transformer reduced it by 0.003293 radians. The current-command Transformer beat carry-forward in nine recordings, losing in f1, f4 and f8. It also beat the camera-only Transformer in nine, losing in f2, f4 and f11. Neither comparison had ties.

Recording identity and inference

The archive reuses local fish numbers across dates. May f7 and f8 are separate files with the same session label. Physical identity across dates and independence within that session remain unresolved. The comparisons describe these recordings and their software-clock alignment. They do not establish a causal response, cross-laboratory performance or an interval of uncertainty for other animals.

Ryosuke Tanaka and Ruben Portugues recorded the source data for Algorithmic dissection of optic flow memory in larval zebrafish. These forecasts and error summaries were calculated from the Figure 4 recordings.
