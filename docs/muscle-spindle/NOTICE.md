# Sources and reuse

## Measured rat recording

Blum KP, Campbell KS, Horslen BC, Nardelli P, Housley SN, Cope TC and Ting LH (2020). *Diverse and complex muscle spindle afferent firing properties emerge from multiscale muscle mechanics*. eLife 9, e55177. The replay uses the experimental archive `spindle_data.zip`, member `eLife_data/aff015_proc.mat`, MATLAB trial 1 (`trad_series`). Its SHA-256 is `b3312ecf0a5840d96336a309a26afaaa4d1396107dc20035d58f2a65d3b56a23`. The Dryad dataset (published version 2, version ID 95220) is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. [Article](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.55177) · [Dataset](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.vdncjsxsw) · [Zenodo recording and simulation archives](https://zenodo.org/records/4315453).

The source label identifies one passive stretch of the left triceps surae muscle group of a healthy adult female Wistar rat. A group Ia sensory axon supplied the recorded spikes. The force and position amplitudes retain the source scale. No determination of intrafusal force, absolute membrane voltage, muscle fibre displacement or subjective sensation follows from these signals. Afferent prefixes describe groups of archive filenames. Their correspondence with individual animals is uncertain.

`extract-trial.py` reads the displayed arrays from the original, SHA-checked MATLAB member. It preserves double precision and expresses spike times in seconds. Rebuild with `python extract-trial.py aff015_proc.mat` in an environment with NumPy and SciPy. The mechanics, electrical samples and detected events can each be saved as a separate CSV. The authors recorded inside a single dorsal-root sensory axon. The displayed electrical amplitude has no established absolute voltage calibration. Forecasters and their evaluation are described in the [NeuroTranslation study](https://eliminatenoise.github.io/NeuroTranslation/papers/muscle-spindle/).

## Original schematic and software

The Interoceptomics cutaway shows five intrafusal fibres with branched primary endings and an open capsule in parallel with extrafusal bundles. The number of fibres and terminal turns is illustrative. The model omits secondary and motor endings, nuclei, fine capsule layers and much of the muscle. The spatial dimensions of the spindle and its nerve are exaggerated relative to the surrounding unit. The photographs supply anatomical references from different specimens. The axon recording has no matched images or geometry.

The transverse view intersects the same triangles with a plane across the middle. The retained half of the capsule and surrounding bundles keep the cutaway open. The GLB and the interactive drawing use the same original mesh generator. Run `node export-glb.cjs` in the reader folder to rebuild the GLB. A glTF reader uses metres, but the model's dimensions have no specimen-specific calibration. The reader, original geometry, generated model and original source code are offered under the project's CC0 1.0 dedication to the extent the project holds rights.
