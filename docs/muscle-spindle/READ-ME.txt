Inside a muscle spindle — Interoceptomics

Extract the whole ZIP into a folder. Open index.html in a current browser, or use
Open on Windows.cmd, Open on macOS.command or Open on Linux.sh. No installer,
server or network connection is needed for the cutaway, recording or forecasts.
On a desktop that asks how to open a shell script, open index.html directly.

Drag the model or use the arrow keys with the model focused. Select a structure
to locate it. Use the time slider to count detected events and compare the five
past intervals with the following 20 ms. The full-trial plot also accepts clicks.
Save mechanics and spike times from Recording, clocks and reuse. The electrical
trace also exports all native electrical samples.

muscle-spindle.glb opens in a glTF 2.0 viewer. It is an original anatomical
schematic with exaggerated proportions. The two photographs are observations
from other rats. NOTICE.md credits the data and images and explains reuse.

The study, external source links and the wider atlas require network access.
