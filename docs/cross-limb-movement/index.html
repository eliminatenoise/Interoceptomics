Where the measurements came from

Right wrist and five right muscles

Luciw, Jarocka & Edin measured the wrist with a Polhemus sensor during right-hand grasp-and-lift trials. The stored P4 coordinates are in centimetres. The authors’ wrist-speed calculation and Figure 2 establish the unit. The viewer converts displacement to millimetres. The conversion from the released muscle amplitudes to volts is unknown.

Sacrum and eight leg muscles

Peterson & Ferris measured a reflective marker over the sacrum while a person walked heel to toe. The cameras recorded at 100 Hz and the leg electrodes at 1,000 Hz. The released streams share a 256 Hz clock after the authors’ processing. The leg amplitudes are in microvolts (µV).
