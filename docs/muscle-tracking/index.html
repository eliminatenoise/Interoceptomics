Inside a lifting foot

Lift the front of your foot. A muscle beside your shinbone pulls on a tendon that crosses the ankle. An ultrasound probe can follow a bundle of fibres inside that muscle as the foot moves.

What did the tracker measure?

A fascicle is a bundle of muscle fibres. The study tracked how far that bundle ran between two sheets of connective tissue and the angle it made with the central sheet. That tilt is its pennation angle.

The drawing lays the central sheet flat and draws one straight segment from the two readings. Its origin and orientation on the page are chosen for comparison. The recording contains no three-dimensional coordinates for these endpoints.

Read the evidence

Participants lifted the forefoot while seated, with the foot suspended. The curves connect successive tracked estimates. The length and angle come from the same images and can share tracking error. Neither supplies muscle force. The mapping from these tracking steps to the study’s electrical and ankle-motion samples remains unresolved.
