Between two pressure readings

Brierley SM and Brizuela M (2026). Electrophysiological recordings of mouse
colonic afferent responses to mechanical and pharmacological stimuli in ex vivo
preparations. SPARC dataset 659, version 1. CC BY 4.0.
https://doi.org/10.26275/UZOC-EOK4
https://discover.pennsieve.io/datasets/659

The source file is files/primary/sub-R481/sam-R481/sam-R481.smr.
The published object's version ID is bgu_LkC1yT3fA6OcBlw2BWgHITij6yA5.
The provider lists SHA-256 a4c9fe6fcd215bc02f4ffce8264965a1edfdc7c9b65cb15178770a9b9b2c7183.

The examples use the first stored mark on each of channels 6 and 7 in R481.
Channel numbers start at one. Each mark has two neighbouring samples
in one continuous block of the pressure recording on channel 3. The table
gives ticks since the recording began. Each tick is 8 microseconds.

  Mark       Nerve channel   Earlier sample   Nerve event   Later sample
  Earlier         6                12504          17032           25004
  Later           7               887504         888120          900004

The two event markers begin at byte offsets 239862292 and 251802132.
The corresponding pairs of pressure samples begin at 5142 and 5144,
and at 5282 and 5284. Byte offsets start at zero.

Channel 3 stores a signed 16-bit count for each reading, with the low byte
first. Its scale is 100, its offset is 0 and its unit is mmHg (millimetres
of mercury). The Spike2 waveform conversion is

  pressure in mmHg = count × scale / 6553.6 + offset

One count on this channel equals 0.0152587890625 mmHg on the file's scale.

  Mark       Counts before / after    Before (mmHg)     After (mmHg)
  Earlier             3 / 2           0.0457763671875   0.030517578125
  Later               2 / 4           0.030517578125    0.06103515625

The reader converts ticks to seconds and places each nerve mark between its
two pressure samples. Subtracting the times gives the two intervals in
milliseconds. On the diagram, pressure values are rounded to three decimal
places. The exact conversions, sample times and byte offsets are in
pressure-brackets.json.

The decimals above follow from the file's scale. The recording header,
dataset readme and study protocol give no comparison to a known pressure
for this recording, so the gauge's absolute accuracy is unknown. The
pressure at either nerve mark, the nerve fibre, the kind of response, the
treatment and the stage of the trial are also unknown.

Cambridge Electronic Design documents the conversion under "Waveform
channels" in its Spike2 version 8 manual. NeuralEnsemble's reader for this
file format implements the same conversion.
https://ced.co.uk/img/Spike8print.pdf
https://github.com/NeuralEnsemble/python-neo/blob/e899f3e1e13e11ed09045c463ee340ac86f98311/neo/rawio/spike2rawio.py
