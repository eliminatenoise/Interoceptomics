The examples use the first stored mark on each of channels 6 and 7 in R481.
Channel numbers start at one. Each mark has two neighbouring samples
in one continuous block of the pressure recording on channel 3. The table
gives ticks since the recording began. Each tick is 8 microseconds.

  Mark       Nerve channel   Earlier sample   Nerve event   Later sample
  Earlier         6                12504          17032           25004
  Later           7               887504         888120          900004

The two event markers begin at byte offsets 239862292 and 251802132.
The corresponding pairs of pressure samples begin at 5142 and 5144,
and at 5282 and 5284. Byte offsets start at zero.

The reader converts ticks to seconds and places each event on a time
axis spanning its two pressure samples. It subtracts the event and sample
times to show the two distances in milliseconds. The file coordinates
identify the samples without retrieving either pressure value. Pressure
between readings, fibre identity, treatment and trial phase remain unknown.
