Between two pressure readings

Researchers recorded pressure inside an isolated mouse colon and electrical activity in its attached pelvic nerve. Both recordings share a clock. What did the gauge read just before and just after a mark in the nerve recording?

Choose a marked nerve event

The scale saved in the file converts the gauge’s counts to mmHg. The display rounds the results to three decimal places. This recording gives no check against a known pressure, so the gauge’s accuracy is unknown.

What was the pressure at the nerve mark?

The clock counted in steps of 8 microseconds. The gauge took a reading every 100 milliseconds (ms). Both nerve marks fell in the gap between readings, so the pressure at the instant of each mark is unknown.

About the recording

Brierley and Brizuela deposited this recording from mouse R481. Each example uses the first mark saved on one of two nerve-event channels. For each mark, the nerve fibre, the kind of response, the treatment and the stage of the trial are unknown.
