# Backslash of the Brain

A muscle stretches. An artery expands. The signals enter the nervous system by different routes and can influence the same response. Follow nine circuits from receptor to relay to motor target across a human brain, labelled donor sections, spinal cord and organ surfaces.

Extract the ZIP and start **Linux.sh**, **macOS.command** or **Windows.cmd** with Python 3.10 or newer installed. The launcher opens the model locally and prints its address. Use the four views to explore freely or choose a route under **Follow a circuit**. The illustrated atlas inside the package gives directions that work offline.

The 3D brain uses left-sided Allen atlas anatomy. Thirty coronal label sections expose ten landmarks, and separate right-donor brainstem sections show structures too small for those surface meshes. The cord and organs occupy the Human Reference Atlas coordinate system. Diagrammed connections and role colours are explanatory. The models contain no recordings of neural activity.

The project’s original explanatory additions use CC0. The Allen and HRA surfaces and the published plates retain their source terms.
