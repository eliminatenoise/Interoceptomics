Open the Interoceptomics stage guide

  1. Extract the entire ZIP before opening the guide.
  2. Open Windows.cmd, macOS.command or Linux.sh in the extracted folder. You need Python 3.10 or newer and a current browser.
  3. Leave the launcher window open. If the guide doesn’t appear, paste the address printed there into your browser. When you finish, press Ctrl+C in the launcher window.

After launch, choose Technical guide or Simple guide at the top of the experiment guide.

Follow one experiment

Choose Human movement for the wrist-pointing trial. Move through the recording to read the wrist angles and scalp and forearm signals. The separate Task time slider moves the illustrated limb through a programmed pose.

Choose Animal learning, then 0.2 · Navigation. Use Camera frame to follow measured positions of a rat on a W-shaped track. The straight-corridor dot below follows a programmed path. The two controls have separate clocks.

Choose Animal learning. Under Stage or task, choose Back to human function. This opens Myoception. Under Model to explore, choose Trace a signal from a muscle, then Open here. The Backslash atlas and its illustrated PDF are included. Its circuits show anatomy and proposed signal routes. The models contain no recording of nerve activity.

Chest vibration at rest

In the Stage Guide, open Visceroception and choose Compare chest-vibration forecasts at rest. Choose Open here to compare errors for seven people. The version 1 recorded example is also included.

Play NeuroSurge Lite offline

While online, download NeuroSurge Lite. Leave NeuroSurge-Lite.zip zipped. Put it beside this file or beside the extracted Stage Guide folder. Reopen the guide launcher, choose Enter a virtual challenge and select Open NeuroSurge in a separate tab. Press F to type a movement. Press C for camera controls. The browser requests video after you select Start camera.

Offline use and online links

You can use all 50 guide steps, their recording excerpts, the included readers and the two script PDFs offline. In Large Movement Models, choose Pinch and hook forecasts and select Open here to use the bundled forecaster. Original publications and other web links need internet access. The recorded-cortex reader can optionally load a participant’s cortical surface from the source or from a verified local file.
