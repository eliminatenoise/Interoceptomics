## Turn the left red nucleus

Choose **Red nucleus · left atlas surface**, then **Isolate in place**. The surrounding midbrain disappears and you can turn the nucleus. The medial overview sits below the model in a narrow window and beside it in a wide window. The anatomical axes stay on the model. **Restore context** brings the surrounding brain back.

## Open the preview

Extract the whole ZIP and open its README. The Windows, macOS and Linux launchers need Python 3.10 or newer and a browser with WebGL. The canvas takes its size when the viewer opens. Reload after resizing if the model looks cropped.

## What the atlas shows

R10 added the unsmoothed left red nucleus surface from Allen Human Reference Atlas — 3D, 2020 v1.0.0, label 12247 on a 0.5 mm grid. This is the original left atlas. Comparison with separate MRI and histology is qualitative. Quantitative anatomy and measured neural function need separate evidence. The AMD/RADV C01 path, native Windows/macOS launch, held-out anatomy, full appearance and export acceptance remain open.
