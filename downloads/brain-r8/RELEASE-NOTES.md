## Two neighbours inside the thalamus

Look for **VPM** under **Separate meshes**. Choose **Isolate in place**, then drag to turn it. Choose **Focus** to centre it again and **Restore context** to bring the hemisphere back. Search for **VPL** next to see the adjoining territory.

This left atlas surface follows Allen Human Reference Atlas — 3D, 2020 v1.0.0 label 10425. Its 1,016 labelled voxels occupy 127 mm³ on the 0.5 mm grid. VPM follows the inner edge of VPL in this atlas. Its centre lies 5.39 mm closer to the midline than VPL’s centre, counting the labelled voxel centres in each territory. The atlas names a parvocellular division (VPMpc) without giving it separate voxels in version 1. The surface gives one atlas boundary. Individual anatomy and neural function require separate evidence.
